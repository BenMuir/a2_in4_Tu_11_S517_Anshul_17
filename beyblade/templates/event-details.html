Event Overview & Schedule

Welcome to the official Brisbane Premier 3v3 tournament. Competitors must register a legal deck of 3 distinct Beyblade X setups with no duplicate parts across blades, ratchets, or bits. Standard WBBA/WBO deck rules apply.

  • 09:30 AM: Registration check-in and launcher speed calibration.
  • 10:00 AM: Deck inspection and Round 1 bracket pools start.
  • 01:30 PM: Finals showcase and prize ceremony.
