Acknowledgement of Country

An Acknowledgement of Country is a way of showing respect for the Traditional Owners and Custodians of the land where an event takes place, and recognising their continuing connection to land, waters and culture. Every X-Burst host can add one to their tournament page.

Why it matters

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples have cared for Country for tens of thousands of years. Acknowledging this at the start of an event recognises that history and its ongoing importance, and is a small, everyday part of reconciliation: building respectful relationships between First Nations peoples and other Australians.

Acknowledgement vs Welcome to Country
Welcome to Country Acknowledgement of Country
Who gives it Only a Traditional Owner or Custodian of that land Anyone, Indigenous or non-Indigenous
What it is Traditional Owners welcoming visitors onto their Country, sometimes with a speech, song, dance or smoking ceremony A statement of respect for the Traditional Owners of the land you are on
On X-Burst Must be arranged directly with the local Traditional Owners; it can't be added through this site Hosts can add one to any tournament page
